# Risk criteria of this repository

This file is part of a live check of requirement #102 of cumin-works. It is
removed at the end of the check.

| Risk | Criteria |
|---|---|
| `risk/low` | A change that only this sandbox can see. |
| `risk/high` | Anything that a revert cannot undo. |
| `risk/medium` | Everything that is not low or high. |
